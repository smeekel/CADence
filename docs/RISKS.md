# Risk register {#risks}

Risks to delivering S0 … S10 as planned in `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.md`. Likelihood and impact are High,
Medium or Low. "Owner" is the step or spike that retires or controls the risk; spikes are time-boxed and end in the
ADR named. Review this table at every stage gate (`Sx.99.3`) and add a row whenever a step turns up a new risk.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | **Topological naming robustness.** References to faces and edges re-attach to the wrong entity after upstream edits (A-06), silently corrupting models. | High | High | Spike SP-2 compares naming schemes on the 20 classic breakage cases before any feature code; `TopoRef` records feature ID, generation role and geometric signature; failures report "reference lost" rather than guess; the 20-case golden suite runs on every build from S2. | SP-2 (S0.09.5, S0.09.6, ADR 0035); S0.09.7; S2.06.1, S2.06.2 |
| R-02 | **OCCT fillet and shell failures** on valid-looking inputs (variable fillets, shells through fillets, thin walls). | High | Medium | Kernel errors become feature error states with messages (P-21), never crashes; golden failure cases in the applied-feature suite; fallback options (setback, fewer faces) surfaced to the user; OCCT version pinned and upgraded only with the golden suite green. | S2.04.1, S2.04.2, S2.04.4, S2.04.10 |
| R-03 | **Sketch solver convergence**: divergence, slow drag on large sketches, poor redundancy/conflict reports (P-13: under 16 ms per drag). | Medium | High | Spike SP-3 prototypes graph decomposition plus damped Newton / Levenberg–Marquardt on a 500/800 system before design; T-10 solver suite and the P-13 benchmark gate the solver; SolveSpace or PlaneGCS kept as a CR-07 fallback reference. | SP-3 (S1.01.2, S1.01.3, ADR 0036); S1.03.10, S1.03.11 |
| R-04 | **Mate solver performance** on large or over-constrained assemblies; slow drag, wrong DOF counts. | Medium | High | Spike SP-4 on the 15 golden mechanisms; rigid-cluster reduction; incremental solving; early measurement on 2,000 parts (SP-8a). | SP-4 (S3.01.2, S3.01.3, ADR 0037); S3.02.4; S3.03.7 |
| R-05 | **Push-and-shove router complexity**: interactive routing misses P-15 (under 50 ms per mouse move) or leaves DRC errors. | High | High | Spike SP-5 on a dense synthetic board; router core exposed as services; shove suite with zero-DRC acceptance; published algorithms or a CR-07-recorded GPL-compatible reference if fresh work stalls. | SP-5 (S5.01.2, S5.01.3, ADR 0038); S5.09.9, S5.09.10 |
| R-06 | **Polygon pour robustness**: slivers, islands, thermal-relief errors, slow re-pour on dense boards. | Medium | High | Spike SP-6 on Clipper2 with dense boards; integer nm grid; area, thermal and clearance golden tests; auto re-pour deferred to S10 as a background job. | SP-6 (S5.01.4, S5.01.5, ADR 0039); S5.11.5; S10.06.8 |

| R-08 | **Cross-domain link semantics**: cycles, update storms or unreviewed propagation between board and enclosure. | Medium | High | Spike SP-7 prototypes link nodes across documents with review mode; cycles rejected at creation; change log and review mode before automatic propagation. | SP-7 (S6.01.1, S6.01.2, ADR 0040); S6.02.4 |
| R-09 | **C++26 compiler support gaps**: features MSVC accepts but GCC/Clang lack (L-03), or MSVC regressions. No native GCC/Clang on the reference machine. | Medium | Medium | Spike SP-1 builds a feature matrix (WSL 2 compilers for GCC/Clang); `docs/cpp-features.md` allow-list enforced by a check; feature-test macros with fallbacks in `core/Compat.h`. | SP-1 (S0.01.2, ADR 0034); S0.01.5 |
| R-10 | **Qt + vcpkg build times**: first builds compile Qt and OCCT from source (hours); later Chrono and Embree add more; slow clean-clone verification and hosted CI. | High | Medium | Headless installs only test dependencies; `qtbase` with minimal features; vcpkg binary cache configured in SP-1; heavy ports added only in the stage that needs them (Clipper2 S5, Chrono and ngspice S9, Embree S10). | S0.01.1 (ADR 0029); S1.08.1 |
| R-11 | **ngspice and Chrono integration**: ports may not build with the pinned baseline; API changes; Chrono build size. | Medium | Medium | Both confined to `sim`; ngspice loaded dynamically through `platform`; overlay ports recorded in `docs/provenance.md` if needed; checked at the start of S9 with a free-fall and an RC smoke test before deeper work. | S9.01.1 (ADR 0007); S9.03.1 (ADR 0008) |
| R-12 | **ngspice licence unclear**: the vcpkg port metadata declares CC-BY-SA-4.0; parts of ngspice historically had other terms. | Medium | High | Verify the licence of the exact source before linking or shipping; exclude incompatible parts or supersede ADR 0008. | S9.03.1 (ADR 0008) |
