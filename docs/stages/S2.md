## Key risks

- R-01: Topological naming robustness. References to faces and edges re-attach to the wrong entity after upstream edits (A-06), silently corrupting models (see `docs/RISKS.md`).
- R-02: OCCT fillet and shell failures on valid-looking inputs (variable fillets, shells through fillets, thin walls) (see `docs/RISKS.md`).
- R-13: File-format evolution (see `docs/RISKS.md`).
- R-19: Third-party STEP import variability (see `docs/RISKS.md`).
- R-20: Golden data maintenance (see `docs/RISKS.md`).
